Secrets stay outside Git. Documents are private. Client/admin tokens must not enter localStorage. Mobile sensitive tokens use SecureStore. Frontend route guards do not replace backend authorization. Do not invent legal policies, launch claims, store links, company addresses, credentials or account identifiers.
Keep docs/IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md accurate, distinguishing implemented, tested, pending and externally blocked. Production/TestFlight readiness requires release gates, not merely source files or a passing bundle export.
