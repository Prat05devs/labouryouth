# Implementation decisions and open release inputs
The user-provided specification is authoritative. This log resolves implementation details without adding features.

1. Headcount: capacity is `headcount`; an accepted offer consumes one open slot. For headcount=1 the first accept wins and all other pending offers withdraw. For headcount>1 acceptance continues until capacity fills; then withdraw the rest. Lock the parent job and accepting worker. A job becomes ASSIGNED when capacity is full; partially filled requests remain OFFERING while existing assignments may proceed. This interprets “winner” without losing the explicitly required multi-worker capability.
2. Scheduling: explicit shift windows are the Phase 1 execution contract for all four engagement types. HOURLY/DAILY have a single window; FIXED_TERM/MONTHLY have a bounded, validated list plus recurrence metadata/timezone for future generation. No unbounded recurring generation tonight. Every recurring assignment gets multiple Shift rows. UI must collect shift times, not guess 24-hour days. PostgreSQL timestamps are UTC; recurrence metadata keeps IANA timezone.
3. Availability: ONLINE means accepting immediate offers; it is not verification and does not cancel accepted work. Scheduled availability and engagement lists constrain future shifts. Recheck schedule conflicts at acceptance.
4. Verification: operations explicitly approves required IDENTITY and POLICE records; DRIVER also needs DRIVING_LICENSE where configured in service requirements. Worker ACTIVE additionally requires completed onboarding and an explicit authorized activation decision. Approval is never inferred from upload existence. Requirement policy is data/config driven.
5. Quote-on-request is valid. Missing rate is not zero pay. Accepted offers freeze agreed worker earning amount in integer paise; no automatic earnings without confirmed commercial terms. Client payment settlement remains independent.
6. Admin “manual assign” means selecting eligible candidate and sending an offer, followed by worker acceptance. An exceptional forced assignment would require an explicit future policy; do not silently bypass worker consent.
7. Manual attendance: worker requests review with reason; operations approves/rejects with reason. Approval creates MANUAL_CHECK_IN and audit record; original failed observations are preserved. There is no client-side bypass.
8. Complete-shift is atomic and idempotent. It creates at most one earning per shift when amount is agreed; future confirmation/disputes can use COMPLETION_PENDING. Assignment completes after all non-cancelled shifts complete; job completes after fulfillment of all required slots/shifts, including replacements.
9. Release blockers are visible, never faked: production host/DB/storage/email, Apple membership/identifiers/signing, Expo project, official contact/store links, reviewed privacy/terms/worker policy and device verification. Development defaults cannot silently ship.
10. Worker availability setup saves the chosen service area in OFFLINE mode without requiring GPS. Foreground location is required when switching ONLINE or checking in for a shift; switching OFFLINE preserves the last known point without requesting permission. This avoids blocking resumable onboarding on a permission unrelated to the current action.

Web UI uses Tailwind v4 + shadcn/ui (new-york) so the same components carry into the product; legacy admin/info CSS is scoped under .legacy until migrated. Photos are Unsplash (free licence) stored locally in apps/web/public/img; no arch/pill-shaped cards.

Changes to these choices must update related contracts, state tables and tests. Remaining exact rate cards, required evidence policies, retention periods and operational contact information are owner configuration inputs, not reasons to invent scope.
