# Competitor research (3 October 2026)
Purpose: inform the guest-first experience (Decision 18). Figures are the competitors' own or press-reported claims at the time of reading, not verified by us. We copy patterns, never claims.

## What was found
- **Urban Company** (urbancompany.com): the homepage and category pages are viewable without login, with location-based listings (Dehradun appears in the page title) and a "Register as a professional" link. Booking is category, then a standardized service with a fixed price and a time slot.
- **Snabbit** (snabbit.com): leads with speed and price clarity ("about 10 minutes", hourly rates from Rs 99, no hidden charges). Trust claims are concrete: a 2-day training, background and Aadhaar verification before a first booking, a refund promise, an SOS feature. Press reports a micromarket density strategy and worker-side strain (pay depends on investor-funded pricing, workers juggle many households).
- **Apna, WorkIndia, QuikrJobs** (blue-collar job apps): free search and apply for job seekers, direct employer contact, category and city browsing, multilingual support, sign-up by missed call on QuikrJobs.

## What we adopted
- Open the app on browsable content, not a login wall (Urban Company pattern).
- A category icon grid as the main entry (Urban Company), driven by the database catalog.
- Price (daily wage) shown large on every job card before sign-up (Snabbit pattern).
- Role split at the top: "I want work" and "I want to hire".
- Honest trust signals only: verified by our team, wage shown up front, free for workers.

## What we deliberately did not copy
- Refund guarantees, SOS, arrival-time promises, customer counts, ratings volume: we do not offer or have them. Owner rule: no invented claims.
- Discount-led pricing and worker pay that depends on subsidies.

## Gaps to consider next (owner decisions)
- Missed-call or phone-OTP sign-up for low-literacy users (QuikrJobs); current release has no OTP by design.
- Hindi-first default for Dehradun rather than English.
- Contact policy: Apna-style direct contact is not offered; contact only after a selection.
